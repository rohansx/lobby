Changelog

What changed in Lobby, newest first. Every change is also on GitHub.

29 October 2026

Docs, and sign in with Google or GitHub

  • New documentation: quick start, embedding, the line and referrals, invites, email, the REST API, webhooks, the CLI and self-hosting.
  • Sign in or sign up with Google or GitHub. They also work on self-hosted instances, for the admin.
  • Lobby Cloud moved to trylobby.app, with the dashboard, API and hosted pages on one domain.
  • Choosing a plan on the pricing page now takes you through sign-up straight to checkout.
28 October 2026

Lobby Cloud New

  • Hosted Lobby: free for 500 signups, Launch from $4 a month and Pro from $10 a month. See pricing.
  • Accounts with email confirmation and password reset, usage meters, and billing through Polar.
  • A new logo, landing page and pricing page, and the dashboard restyled to match.
27 October 2026

Docker image and landing page

  • Multi-architecture image published to ghcr.io/rohansx/lobby from CI.
  • README with a demo and screenshots, and the first landing page.
26 October 2026

First release

  • A self-hosted waitlist for many projects: widget, plain HTML form and hosted page.
  • Double opt-in, places in line, referral points, invite waves, broadcasts and signed webhooks.
  • A dashboard, a REST API, a CLI and /llms.txt for coding agents.
  • Emails that render in every client, and an email to you for every new signup.
