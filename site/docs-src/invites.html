Sending invites

On the project's Overview, Invite the next wave lets in the next N people in line. On Signups you can select specific people and invite them instead. From code:

curl -X POST https://trylobby.app/v1/invites \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_…" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"count": 50}'        # or {"ids": [12, 31, 40]}

Each invited person moves to invited and gets a "You're in" email with two ways to claim their spot:

  • a magic link to your Invite URL with ?wl=<token> added, for example https://orbit.app/signup?wl=…,
  • an invite code like K7PD-3XQM, bound to their email address, for people who sign up some other way.

Without an Invite URL, the link opens their status page.

Redeeming in your app

When someone arrives with ?wl=, or types a code at signup, check it with Lobby from your server:

const res = await fetch("https://trylobby.app/v1/invites/redeem", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.LOBBY_KEY}`, "Content-Type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({ token: searchParams.get("wl") }),
  // or: JSON.stringify({ email, code })
})
if (res.status === 404) throw new Error("That invite isn't valid or was already used.")
const person = await res.json() // the signup, now with status "joined"

A valid invite is marked used and the person becomes joined. Each invite works once. An unknown, revoked or used invite answers 404.

Not using invite links? Call POST /v1/signups/{id}/joined when someone from the list signs up for your product, so your numbers stay right.

Launch day broadcast

Broadcast emails everyone in an audience at once: confirmed, invited, or all (confirmed, invited and joined). People who unsubscribed are always skipped, and every broadcast carries one-click unsubscribe headers that Gmail and Apple Mail show as an Unsubscribe button.

curl -X POST https://trylobby.app/v1/broadcasts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_…" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"subject": "Orbit is live", "body": "Thanks for waiting…", "audience": "all"}'

Broadcasts are queued and sent in the background. On Lobby Cloud they count toward your plan's monthly emails, and one that would go over is refused before anything is sent.

A launch checklist

  1. Set the Invite URL and test redeeming an invite on a staging copy of your app.
  2. Self-hosting? Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC for your sending domain (see Email).
  3. Invite a small first wave, say 50, and watch for bounces and replies.
  4. Increase the waves as your product holds up.
  5. When you open to everyone, send a broadcast to all.
