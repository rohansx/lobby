1. Create an account and a project

  1. Sign up with Google, GitHub or an email and password. Email sign-ups get a confirmation link first.
  2. Click + next to Projects in the sidebar and give the waitlist a name, for example Orbit. The slug (orbit) becomes the address of its hosted page.
  3. On the project's Overview, the Add it to a site card has every snippet with your keys filled in. The secret key for the API is shown once, right after you create the project. Lost it? Rotate secret key on the Keys card gives you a new one.

2. Put the form on your site

Paste this where you want the form to appear, with your project's public key:

<div id="waitlist"></div>
<script defer src="https://trylobby.app/v1/wl.js"
  data-project="pk_…" data-target="#waitlist" data-counter></script>

That's a complete signup form with a live "1,204 people waiting" counter. No site yet? Share the hosted page instead: https://trylobby.app/w/orbit.

Lock the form to your domain: in Settings → Allowed origins, add https://orbit.app. Until you add one, any site can post to your list.

3. Try it

  1. Sign up on your page with a real inbox you can check.
  2. Click the confirmation link in the email. It opens your status page: your place in line and a referral link.
  3. Back in the dashboard, you're on the Signups tab as confirmed, with the page you came from.

4. When you're ready to launch

On the Overview, use Invite the next wave: pick how many people to let in, and Lobby emails each of them an invite link. Set an Invite URL in Settings so the link lands on your product's signup page. Invites and launch day covers the details.
