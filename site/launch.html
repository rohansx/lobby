How it works

Five steps, each one agreed with you.

Describe

Fill in one product profile: what it does, who it's for, pricing, stage, descriptions, screenshots and a demo. Anything missing is listed, never made up.

Review

We check your landing page, demo, signup flow, assets and tracking, and send you a short list of anything that would hold the launch back.

Approve

We propose destinations that suit your product and its stage, each with a reason, its fee and who does the final step. Nothing happens until you approve.

Submit

We do the submissions that may be done for you. Where a platform needs your own account, you get instructions and a checklist, and you post.

Report

Every destination is followed up and recorded. The report shows what was submitted, what went live, and the visits, signups and confirmations from each.

Scope

What you get, and what you don't.

A launch campaign is work we can do and show you. Whether a platform accepts or features your product is their decision, so we promise the work and the evidence, not the outcome.

Included

  • A readiness review with a short list of blockers
  • One approved product profile and asset kit, reused everywhere
  • A shortlist of up to 20 suitable destinations, approved by you
  • Submissions done for you, or guided where you post yourself
  • Submission work within 10 business days of your approval
  • Follow-up tracking for 30 days after that
  • A results report, with evidence for every submission
  • Waitlist limits lifted: unlimited signups, 3 waitlists, webhooks and API

Not promised

  • Acceptance by any platform
  • Rankings, upvotes or a featured spot
  • Backlinks
  • Traffic, signups, customers or revenue
  • When a platform publishes; each sets its own timing
  • A fixed number of destinations; 20 is a maximum, not a quota
The details

Fees, timing and the rules we follow.

Platform fees are separate

Some destinations charge for a listing. Each fee is shown in the plan before you approve it, and you pay it to the platform. It isn't part of the $99, and you can leave any paid destination out.

When a destination isn't available

Directories close, change their rules or turn products away. When that happens, the task records why, and we suggest a suitable replacement if there is one. We don't pad the list to reach a number. Reviews that are still pending at the end are reported as pending.

Your accounts stay yours

We never ask for your passwords. Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit and similar communities need a person posting from their own account, and their rules forbid automated or generated posts. For those you get a checklist, the right timing and reminders, and you write and post.

How results are counted

Each destination gets its own link to your hosted waitlist page. We count visits from those links per day, without storing anything about the visitor, and credit each signup to the source it arrived with. Signups with no source stay "unknown". The report shows counts next to every rate, and says where tracking wasn't possible.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Something else? Email support@trylobby.app.

Who is it for?

Founders and small teams with a working product, or a usable landing page and demo, who are launching soon and would rather not research destinations and fill in the same forms twenty times.

My product isn't live yet. Is it still worth it?

Maybe. Many destinations only list products people can use. For a pre-launch product the plan is smaller and limited to places that accept that stage. The readiness review will tell you if it's worth waiting.

Do I need a Lobby waitlist?

Yes: it's how the campaign is measured. You can use an existing Lobby waitlist or make one during intake. It's included.

When does the clock start?

The 10 business days for submission work start when you approve the plan. Follow-up tracking runs for 30 days after that, and the report comes at the end.

Can I get a refund?

Email support@trylobby.app. A person reads every request and replies.

I'm on a monthly Launch or Pro plan.

Nothing changes for you. Your subscription keeps renewing, and you manage it from Billing in the dashboard. You can buy a Launch Pack as well.
