FAQ

Pricing, plainly.

Something else? Open an issue.

Why is self-hosting free?

Lobby is open source under the MIT license. Every feature ships in the binary, with no paid tier and no limits. Cloud exists for people who'd rather not run a server, not to hold features back.

Is Cloud available now?

Cloud is in early access. Join the list and we'll invite people in waves. Until then, self-hosting takes about a minute with Docker.

What counts as an email?

Any email Lobby sends for you: confirmations, status updates, invites and broadcasts. The notifications Lobby sends to you about new signups don't count.

Why are prices this low?

Lobby is a single Go binary with one SQLite file per instance and uses about 20 MB of memory. It costs very little to run, so it costs very little to buy.

Can I leave?

Yes. Export your list as CSV or NDJSON any time, on any plan, and run the same open-source Lobby on your own server.
