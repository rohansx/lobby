What $99 covers

The work, stated plainly.

We promise to complete the work you approve and to show you evidence for it. We can't promise what a platform decides. More on the Launch Pack.

Scope

One product. A readiness review of your landing page, demo, signup flow, assets and tracking. One product profile and asset kit that every submission uses. A shortlist of up to 20 destinations that suit your product and its stage. Twenty is a maximum, not a quota, and a pre-launch product gets a smaller list. Then the submissions, follow-up and a report.

Delivery

Submission work is done within 10 business days of your plan approval. Follow-up tracking runs for 30 days after that. Publication timing depends on each platform, so a review that is still pending at the end is reported as pending.

Platform fees

Some destinations charge for a listing. Those fees aren't part of the $99. Each one is shown in the plan before you approve it, you pay it to the platform, and you can leave any paid destination out.

Unavailable destinations

If a destination closes, changes its rules or turns the product away, the task records what happened and we suggest a suitable replacement where there is one. We don't add destinations just to reach a number.

Not promised

Acceptance, rankings, upvotes, backlinks, traffic, customers or revenue. Communities such as Product Hunt, Hacker News and Reddit need you to post from your own account. We prepare you and remind you, but we don't post for you there, and we never ask for your passwords.

Your waitlist

Buying a pack lifts your waitlist limits for good: unlimited signups, 3 waitlists, 5,000 emails a month, webhooks and the API. They stay after the campaign ends.

FAQ

Pricing, plainly.

Something else? Email support@trylobby.app.

Why one-time and not a subscription?

A launch happens once. You pay for one campaign with a clear end, and keep the waitlist limits afterwards. If you launch another product, buy another pack.

Why is it $99?

It's a pilot price while we deliver the first campaigns by hand. It may change for new purchases. What you've bought doesn't.

What happens at 500 signups on the free waitlist?

Everyone already on your list keeps their place, and the form politely turns new visitors away. We email you when it happens. A Launch Pack removes the limit.

I'm on a monthly Launch or Pro plan.

Nothing changes. Your subscription renews as before, and Manage billing in the dashboard opens Polar's portal to update your card, get invoices or cancel. We just don't sell new subscriptions.

Why is self-hosting free?

Lobby is open source under the MIT license, and every waitlist feature ships in the binary. The Launch Pack is a service run by people, so it's the part we charge for.

Can I leave?

Yes. Export your list as CSV or NDJSON any time, on any plan, and run the same open-source Lobby on your own server.
